How to choose

  • App icon: compare Carbon h + Punh · Simple, Pencil h · Carbon and Punh Done · Yellow in the 16px row. Those three hold up best.
  • Illustration family: Punh Checklist, Punh Peek and Punh Done · Sky share the sky tile, so they work together on onboarding, empty states and stickers.
  • Detail level: the full Punh Punh suits 64px and up; use the Simple or Glyph character below that.
  • The blueprint keeps Punh Punh off urgent alerts, Need help, risk flags and confidential items, so keep one plain mark, such as Hop Tile, for those.
